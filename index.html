Sanket Bhat

Senior Applied Scientist, Amazon · Bellevue, WA

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, where I work on machine learning and LLM-based systems for measuring and improving the quality of conversational AI. Earlier I led NLP teams at ConcertAI and built large-scale optimization solutions at GE Research. I hold a PhD in Industrial Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where my research focused on production and capacity control under seasonal demand.
